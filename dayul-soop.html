DAYUL SOOP CAFE (2026)

Branding Design

Tools: Adobe Illustrator, Adobe InDesign, Procreate, Figma

Branding design of a cafe in the forest, where they wanted people of all age have fun in their own way with nature. Brand image was created through the forest and trees surrounded near cafe.

Cafe Overview

Dayul Soop cafe is in the forest, where the owner wanted people to have a break and healing time outside the city where people all ages can have fun. They wanted to their unique features of mountain view cafe with large area to walk around with nature to stand out. This is where I incorporated the trees, forest and wood as the main design factor to really emphasize the aesthetic of this cafe’s unique features.

477, Dongnam-ro, Buk-gu, Ulsan, Republic of Korea
