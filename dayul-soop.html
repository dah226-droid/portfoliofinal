My Work

DAYUL SOOP CAFE (2026)

Branding Design

Tools: Adobe Illustrator, Adobe InDesign, Procreate, Figma

Branding design of a cafe in the forest, where they wanted people of all age have fun in their own way with nature. Brand image was created through the forest and trees surrounded near cafe.

Cafe Overview

Dayul Soop cafe is in the forest, where the client wanted people to have a break and healing time outside the city where people all ages can have fun. They wanted to their unique features of mountain view cafe with large area to walk around with nature to stand out. This is where I incorporated the trees, forest and wood as the main design factor to really emphasize the aesthetic of this cafe’s unique features.

477, Dongnam-ro, Buk-gu, Ulsan, Republic of Korea

Logos

I created two logos as client’s request, where one shows cafe’s identity and another one showing the building of a main cafe. They wanted it to be fun but minimal at the same time which aligns with the aesthetic of the actual main building.

Logo 1: Brand Identity

Logo 2: Main Cafe Building

Cup Design

With the cup design, I placed main logo at the center to indicate a cafe. I also used the same color scheme as the logo I used for the consistent cafe branding. I added forest illustration under to make cup more fun and shows cafe’s identity.

Back design of the cup, which is the third cup on the image, has the cafe name in English and also a qr code to the cafe website as a client’s request. In Korean, I also wrote cafe, bakery, and cultural space to inform the users about the place they are currently at. I added some foot silhouette to make it more fun and connected with the front side of the cup.

Merch

For Dayul Soop Cafe merch, the client wanted something that users can use in daily lives, which can also use as an advertisement. With various merch ideas, we decided to create tote bag as a first merch considering the budget and in order to advertise we realized tote bag is the best options.

For the design of the merch, I tried my best to emphasize the unique fun features in the cafe and also keep the natural aesthetic and also fun design for all age can use. I decided doodle style is the best way to achieve it. Some of the doodles that I draw to show Dayul Soop Cafe are:

  • Bird house that is displayed around the forest
  • Pond with tadpoles
  • Yellow flowers
  • Trees
  • Toad shaped rock
  • Cafe building
  • Places where customers can drink

Other Designs

Business Card

For the white business card, I used the building logo for the business card as requested by the client. I kept the typeface and the color same as the logo that I used. I was aiming to make the business card very simple but contain all the information that client wanted like their contact info, cafe name, their name, and cafe’s instagram handle.

For the green one, the client wanted his other business to be in it too and not going just focusing on the cafe. However, he still wanted to keep the style of the cafe which I made a decision of keeping the same color and typeface.

Floor Matt

I used the main logo and the typeface on the floor matt for the cafe. I wanted to make this shows the cafe’s identity well, since this is the first thing customer is going to see when they enter the building. I used the background color of the floor matt same as the color from the logo too, so it shows customers that they are entering the right building and welcoming them.

Bathroom Signage

Since the cafe’s theme is nature, client wanted the bathroom signage to blend in with the nature. To meet their wants, I realized there were a lot of leaves around the cafe, so I decided to use a leaf illustration with male and female icon to design the bathroom signage.

Additional Planning

Client wanted customers not only use this Dayul Soop Cafe as a place to drink but also a place to have fun. They wanted this cafe to be more than a cafe and be a cultural space. So we decided to incorporate some activities for customers to do while spending time at this place and feel welcomed.

Activities

Brought an idea of using chalk for people to express their creativity and enjoy the time while in the cafe. This activity is loved by all age range customers since they are able to write or draw whatever they want on the road. These activities are rarely found in South Korea which is a really strong unique feature of this cafe.

Halloween Event

Communicating with the client through virtually, we are currently planning on a halloween event for the october. The images shows halloween decorations that will be used in the cafe.
